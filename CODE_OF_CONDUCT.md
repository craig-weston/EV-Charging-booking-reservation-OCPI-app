# Code of conduct

Depotcharge brings together fleet operators, charge point operators, software companies and
developers, often commercial competitors. We want working on it to be welcoming and productive for
all of them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be constructive: explain the problem, propose a way forward, and accept decisions gracefully.
- Keep commercial discussions out of issues and pull requests, and never share another party's
  confidential information.
- Credit others' work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, threats, doxxing,
and sustained disruption of discussions, in any project space (issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or
GitHub's "Report content" feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban participants who break this code.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (contributor-covenant.org).
